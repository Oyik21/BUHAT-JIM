# TARA JIM Gym Website

## About the Project

The TARA JIM Gym Website is a simple and informative one-page website created to introduce TARA JIM, a premium strength and fitness gym in Mabalacat City, Pampanga, Philippines.

This website helps visitors learn about the gym, explore its facilities and training programs, meet the coaches, and find important information such as opening hours and contact details. It also provides a contact form for people who want to ask questions or join the gym.

## Website Features

* **Home:** Introduces TARA JIM with a hero section, call-to-action buttons, and animated member stats.
* **About:** Shares the gym's mission and vision, and introduces the coaches.
* **Facilities:** Lists strength training, cardio equipment, free weights, personal training, fitness programs, and group training.
* **Projects:** Shows training programs with filter buttons for Strength, Conditioning, and Wellness.
* **Contact:** Provides the address, phone, email, and opening hours, plus a contact form with validation.
* **Dark and Light Mode:** Lets visitors switch themes, and remembers their choice in the browser.
* **Responsive Design:** Supports desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes, with a slide-down menu on phones.
* **Easy Navigation:** Sticky header, smooth scrolling, and a back-to-top button.

## Technologies Used

* **HTML5:** For the website structure.
* **CSS3:** For styling, layout, and media queries.
* **JavaScript:** For interactive features, form validation, and navigation.
* **Google Fonts:** For website typography (Bebas Neue and Inter).

## Purpose of the Website

The purpose of this website is to promote TARA JIM as a fitness destination and give visitors easy access to information about its facilities, training programs, coaches, and contact details.

## Target Users

* Gym members and new visitors
* Fitness enthusiasts and lifters
* People looking for a gym in Mabalacat and Pampanga
* Anyone who wants to start a fitness journey

## Project Location

Mabalacat City, Pampanga, Philippines
